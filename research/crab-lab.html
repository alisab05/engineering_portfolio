CRAB Lab

Compliant leg mechanisms & legged robot locomotion

The CRAB Lab studies how legged and multi-segmented robots move across complex terrain. Much of our work centers on SCUTTLE (also written SCUTL), a centipede-inspired robot that combines body undulation with rigid, C-shaped legs. My work in the lab falls into three pieces.

Multi-Legged Locomotion Study

I'm also part of a larger robophysics study, led by Jianfeng Lin and presented at APS 2026, on how an elongated, multi-segmented robot modeled after centipedes coordinates its body waves and leg stepping to move efficiently. My role has been running experiments: testing different vertical and horizontal body-wave phase lags against the leg gait to see how they affect locomotion performance, work that fed into the group's effective-contact model and geometric-mechanics analysis of the robot's gait.
