Handhold Cover Structural & Weld Validation

Skills used
  • ANSYS Mechanical
  • Modal Analysis
  • Weld Analysis (Solid Weld Approach)
  • Bolted Joint (DBJ) Analysis
  • FEA

This project was completed during an internship. Specific images and data have been withheld or generalized to protect proprietary company information.

Objective

Validate the structural integrity of a redesigned engine component cover through modal, static, bolted joint (DBJ), and weld analysis, after a new electronic control module mounting bracket was added to it.

Approach

  • Ran modal analysis to check natural frequencies against the operating speed range, and static structural analysis under shock and operational loads
  • Performed bolted joint (DBJ) analysis to evaluate bolt margins under load
  • Extended the analysis with solid weld modeling to validate root, toe, and throat stresses on the welded joints, learning a new weld-analysis methodology in the process

Result

The cover design passed modal, static, and weld analysis with low risk of failure. DBJ analysis flagged some bolt margins for reevaluation, which I identified and recommended follow-up on. Confirmed no redesign of the cover itself was needed.
