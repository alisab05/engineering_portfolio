QSK78 Bracket Structural Validation

Skills used
  • ANSYS Mechanical
  • Modal Analysis
  • Bolted Joint (DBJ) Analysis
  • FEA

This project was completed during an internship. Specific images and data have been withheld or generalized to protect proprietary company information.

Objective

Validate the structural robustness of a new electronic module bracket through shock, modal, and bolted joint analysis.

Approach

  • Ran static structural, shock load, and modal analysis
  • Performed modal scaling and bolted joint (DBJ) analysis to assess bolt margins under load
  • Identified a bolted joint at medium risk of failure

Result

Recommended increasing fastener length by 10mm, improving thread strip margins across all four bolts. Delivered the full analysis on schedule with a clear, actionable design recommendation.
