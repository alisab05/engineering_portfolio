The Emotional Wrecks Robot

Skills used
  • SolidWorks
  • Arduino/C++
  • House of Quality
  • Function Tree development
  • 3D printing
  • Laser cutting
  • Soldering
  • Woodworking

Objective

Design and fabricate a robot to complete a push, pull, and place task for the ME 2110: Creative Decisions and Design final competition.

Approach

  • Developed the design using a House of Quality, function tree, and specifications sheet to translate task requirements into concrete design constraints (40-second task limit, sub-$120 budget, 12x24x18 in. bounding box)
  • Modeled the mechanism in SolidWorks and programmed the control logic in Arduino C++, integrating an emotion arm mechanism, a mind manual mechanism, a sadness lifting mechanism, and a collection box mechanism into one compact machine
  • Fabricated the robot using 3D printing, laser cutting, soldering, and woodworking, solving challenges around string entanglement, PVC/boxing friction, and packaging everything within the bounding box limit

Result

Placed Third overall out of 66 teams

in the final Design Review, judged by Georgia Tech alumni and industry professionals.
